Talk to someone today

Do I need a doctor's referral?

−

No. Families can book directly by calling +91 769 855 2555 or +91 909 980 2555, or by visiting their nearest centre. Self-referral is welcome for speech delay, signs of autism, developmental delay, ADHD, behavioural concerns and learning difficulties.

What conditions do you work with?

+

We work with autism spectrum disorder, speech and language delays, ADHD, cerebral palsy, Down syndrome, sensory processing difficulties, learning disabilities, hearing impairment, post-stroke rehabilitation, and many more developmental and neurological conditions.

Can we have sessions online if we don't live near a centre?

+

Yes. We offer online therapy sessions for families who cannot visit a centre in person. Our therapists are experienced in delivering effective online sessions for speech therapy, behavioural therapy, counselling, and parent coaching.

How long before we see results?

+

Progress timelines vary by child, diagnosis, and frequency of sessions. Most families notice meaningful changes within 3–6 months. We review every child's goals every six weeks and share a progress report with you at each review.

What part do parents play in therapy?

+

A very important one. Research shows children make the fastest progress when parents reinforce therapy goals at home. Our therapists involve you in each session and provide home practice activities tailored to your daily routine.

Do you offer therapy for adults too?

+

Yes. Our adult programme covers post-stroke rehabilitation, speech and swallowing disorders, hearing care, occupational therapy for functional independence, and counselling. Please contact your nearest centre for a personalised assessment.
